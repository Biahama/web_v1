The established Biahama typefaces, served locally for reliable builds.

Original sources (Google Fonts, retrieved 15 October 2026):
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans

CormorantGaramond[wght].ttf
CormorantGaramond-Italic[wght].ttf
DMSans[opsz,wght].ttf

Converted to WOFF2 with FontTools, retaining Latin, Latin extended,
combining marks, punctuation, currency symbols (including INR), and symbols.
DM Sans optical size is fixed at 14, the Google Fonts default; weight remains
variable. Original outlines are retained. Other scripts use browser fallback.
Both families use the SIL Open Font License 1.1; copies are included here.
